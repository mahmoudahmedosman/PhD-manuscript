\subsection{Implementing a model for spectrophotometric data}

Training a spectrophotometric model is done on two types of dataset. Classical models used in cosmological analyses like SALT2 or SALT3 rely on photometric and spectroscopic data 
obtained separately. Models that aim at refinined the standardization scheme of supernovae rely on spectrophotometric data like the SNfactory data (\cite{snfactory}). The 
SNIFS instrument used for SNfactory allows for us to obtain a series of well calibrated spectral sequences for each observed supernova making the need for separate photometric 
data obselete. Being able to use such datasets in NaCl will then require us to add supplementary information in the model to account for that. \\
