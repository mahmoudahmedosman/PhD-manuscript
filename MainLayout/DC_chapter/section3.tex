\subsection{Parameter reconstruction and covariance matrix}

\noindent Since the pre-DC1 dataset is generated with a model that respects the NaCl constraints, we expect to reconstruct the exact same parameters used in the generation. 
In Figure \ref{fig:sn_pars_wres} we compare the generated and reconstructed parameters averaged over all 100 realizations of pre-DC1. 
We see that the reconstructed parameters match the input parameters within the statistical uncertainties of the reconstruction.\\

\begin{figure}[ht]
    \centering
    %\includegraphics[width=1\textwidth]{MainLayout/DC_chapter/figures/band_resid.pdf}
    \includegraphics[width=1\textwidth]{MainLayout/DC_chapter/figures/sn_pars_wres.png}
    \caption{Weighted residuals of each supernova parameter for 100 realization of pre-DC1}
    \label{fig:sn_pars_wres}
\end{figure}

\noindent We are also interested in comparing the constructed empirical covariance matrix and the Fisher covariance matrix from NaCl. In Figure (ref), we compare the 
output uncertainties ($\sigma_{Fisher}$) from 1 pre-DC1 realization with the uncertainties extracted from the empirical covariance matrix ($\sigma_{Emp}$) constructed from the run 
on the 100 pre-DC1 realizations. We see that for each block of parameters $X_0$, $X_1$ and $c$, $\sigma_{Fisher}$ is equivalent to $\sigma_{Emp}$ and well within 
the statistical uncertainties of $\sigma_{Emp}$. This is also highlighted when we look at their ratios where they are centered around 1. We are now convinced that the NaCl can 
produce unbiased parameters along their uncertainties in a \textsc{Lemaître}-like analysis. \\

\subsection{Distance modulus and cosmological parameters reconstruction}

We use the EDRIS module to determine the distance modulus of the supernovae at the end of the lightcurve fit and model training with NaCl. It's important to note
that we are not assessing the efficiency of EDRIS in reconstructing distances as this has been already established in D. Kuhn's 
PhD thesis\footnote{\url{https://theses.hal.science/tel-05451069}} where it was shown that EDRIS does not bias supernova distances in our setup. If we do find biased distances, then
it would come from NaCl. \\
\noindent The results in Figure \ref{fig:dc1_hubble} show the mean binned distance residuals from 100 realizations of pre-DC1 as well as a comparison between between
the empirical uncertainties on the binned distances and the Fisher uncertainties from EDRIS. We see that NaCl does not introduce biases on the supernova distances.
We can now move on to determining the cosmological parameters with cosmologix. \\
